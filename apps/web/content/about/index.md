**Hollowfort** is a web-first dark fantasy deckbuilder. Explore a handcrafted prison, fight with combo-driven cards, and grow class experience as you play.

## The Beta promise

One complete adventure — Hollowfort Prison — that demonstrates exploration, combat, deckbuilding, progression, rewards, and story.

## Architecture

Gameplay rules live in a framework-independent TypeScript engine. The Next.js shell hosts the public site and mounts the game at `/play`.
