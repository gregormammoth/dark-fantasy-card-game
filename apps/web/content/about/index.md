**Hollowfort** is a web-first dark fantasy deckbuilder. Explore a handcrafted prison, fight with combo-driven cards, and grow both class experience and player-level combat skills as you play.

## A prison built on executions

Hollowfort stands on ground soaked by political killings. Decades ago anarchists and other prisoners were put to death within its walls — buried beneath the fortress, their souls left unrested.

One night a mysterious sorcerer completes a forbidden ritual. The dead rise, the guards are slaughtered, and every cell is thrown open.

## Your place in the night

Caught in the massacre, you must survive, escape, and choose where your loyalties lie. Anarchists, surviving Guards, and the Sorcerer's followers all want your help at the Exit Gate.

## What you can play today

- **Sixteen rooms** from Prison Cell to Exit Gate, including three faction branches
- **Five classes** — Warrior, Rogue, Wizard, Survivor, and Seeker — hybridised through one deck
- **Thirty player cards** (fifteen base, fifteen improved unlocks)
- **Player skills** — raise max shield, combo, mana, deck, or draw per turn
- **Quests and crowns** — faction kill-quests, an ingredient errand, and a Smuggler shop
- **Cloud save** for a guest run, plus seeded RNG for reproducible play

## The Beta promise

One complete adventure — Hollowfort Prison — that demonstrates exploration, combat, deckbuilding, progression, rewards, and story. Remaining Beta work is polish, balance, deploy, and feedback — not a new combat system.

## Architecture

Gameplay rules live in a framework-independent TypeScript engine. The Next.js shell hosts the public site and mounts the game at `/play`.
