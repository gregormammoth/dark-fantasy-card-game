---
title: The Prison Slice Is Playable
description: Hollowfort now runs as one continuous escape — cell to gate, cards as life, loyalty as the key.
date: 2026-08-13
---

The public site and `/play` client now describe the same game that ships in the engine.

You wake in a cell, spend cards as exploration actions, and carry that hand into battle. Class XP unlocks improved cards. Every five class levels grant a player level — spend it on shield, combo, mana, deck size, or draw. Crowns pay the Smuggler. Faction quests decide who meets you at the Exit Gate.

What remains for Beta is shipping work: a public deploy, a feedback form, a balance pass, and a full seeded journey test. The loop itself is already one run.
