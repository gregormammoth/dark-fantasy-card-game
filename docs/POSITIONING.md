# Product Positioning & Market Fit

Honest assessment of audience potential, comparable games, and what would most improve discovery and retention.

Related: [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md) (vertical slice), [MECHANICS.md](./MECHANICS.md) (signature rules), [STORY.md](./STORY.md) / [HOLLOWFORT.md](./HOLLOWFORT.md) (narrative hook).

---

## Snapshot

This project is closer to a **strong vertical-slice prototype** than a breakout product. That is normal and useful at this stage.

**Realistic good outcome:** a niche / cult hit among deckbuilder fans — loyal audience, strong demo conversion, then content expansion.

**Unlikely without an extra hook:** mainstream viral popularity. The genre is crowded; players compare new games to polished giants within minutes.

---

## What already works in our favor

- **Clear product identity:** cards define the character; classes, XP, deckbuilding, exploration, and combat form one continuous run.
- **Commercially legible loop:** explore → encounter → battle → class XP → improve deck → explore more → boss → escape → world map. That is a proven loop family (Slay the Spire–adjacent) with room for a darker, more narrative prison / faction story.
- **Signature mechanics to lean on:** health is the deck; exploration hand / deck can carry into battle; faction loyalty can rewrite the escape.
- **Sensible shipping shape:** content-driven JSON, engine separate from UI, early i18n, web-first for sharing builds and iterating.

---

## Why popularity is hard

- **Crowded comparison space.** Players will measure the game against Slay the Spire, Balatro, Inscryption, and other polished deckbuilders.
- **Need a 5-second hook.** “Dark fantasy deckbuilder” is not enough. The first 10 minutes must make something feel unmistakably *ours* — e.g. deck-as-HP, faction loyalty rewriting escape, or exploration hand carrying into battle.
- **Discovery platform.** Web helps demos, itch, Discord, and early sharing, but many deckbuilders get traction on Steam / mobile. Web can still win if the slice is sticky and the funnel is intentional (demo → waitlist / wishlist / Discord).

## Can it be popular?

| Outcome | Likelihood | What it looks like |
|---------|------------|--------------------|
| Niche / cult favorite | Plausible | Finishable Hollowfort slice, fair difficulty, memorable faction branches, people replay for another loyalty path |
| Broad mainstream hit | Hard | Needs an exceptionally sticky signature loop + strong retention + platform strategy |

Treat **loyal deckbuilder audience + strong demo conversion** as success. Expand content after the slice proves identity.

## Popular / relevant alternatives

| Game | Why people compare |
|------|--------------------|
| **Slay the Spire** | Default deckbuilder benchmark |
| **Balatro** | Proof a card loop can explode with a fresh hook |
| **Inscryption** | Dark narrative + cards; atmosphere adjacent |
| **Monster Train / Monster Train 2** | Deep deckbuilding, strong identity |
| **Across the Obelisk** | Party / deck fantasy co-op niche |
| **Wildfrost** | Charming modern indie deckbuilder |
| **Griftlands** | Negotiation + cards + story runs |
| **Hades** (and similar) | Not cards, but “run → upgrade → story” fantasy players crave |
| **Darkest Dungeon** | Tone / stress / party fantasy — mood neighbor, not same systems |

**Closest mechanical neighbors:** Spire-likes with map exploration.  
**Closest fantasy / mood neighbors:** Inscryption and Darkest Dungeon vibes with a prison uprising story.

Browser / itch deckbuilders also compete for attention in the same discovery funnel as a web demo.

## Positioning line (working)

Use a sharp sentence players can repeat. Drafts to refine as the slice hardens:

1. **Deck is life.** Every card you burn is blood — explore and fight with the same hand.
2. **Pick a side to escape Hollowfort.** Loyalty rewrites who helps you at the gate.
3. **Your classes are your character.** XP unlocks cards; the deck *is* who you became in the run.

Prefer one primary line for marketing; keep the others as supporting beats.

---

## What would most improve “can this be popular?”

1. **One sentence hook** that is unique — not “dark fantasy deckbuilder.”
2. **Make exploration ↔ battle hand continuity feel like the signature**, not a subsystem.
3. **Faction choice that visibly changes the run early** (quests, NPCs at the gate, available paths).
4. **Ship Hollowfort polished enough** that strangers finish it and want a second loyalty path.
5. **Retention basics for Beta:** saves, short session goals, clear next objective, analytics + feedback loop.

---

## Product implications for Beta

Align shipping priorities with market reality (see [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md)):

- Polish the vertical slice over building platform features early.
- Prove the reward loop and enemy pacing before large content expansion.
- Treat story factions and quest visibility as product features, not lore extras.
- Use web deploy for demos and feedback; plan Steam / broader distribution as a later growth lever if the slice lands.

## Beta & social announce

### Core rule for every post

Lead with **one signature line**, not “dark fantasy card game.” Primary for beta:

> **Deck is life.** Every card you burn is blood — explore and fight with the same hand.

Support with one beat: Hollowfort prison escape + faction loyalty. Avoid opening with “Slay the Spire-like” — use comps only later for context, never as the headline.

### What “beta announce” should mean

Do not announce a vague “we’re in beta.” Announce a **clear ask**:

1. **Play the Hollowfort vertical slice** (web link)
2. **Leave feedback** (form / Discord)
3. Optional: **join waitlist / Discord** for next builds

If the slice is not finishable yet, announce a **closed playtest** or **dev diary**, not “public beta.” Overclaiming kills trust.

Position beta as a **finishable niche experiment with a sharp mechanic**, not a platform launch.

### LinkedIn

LinkedIn is weak for gamers, strong for **builders, indie founders, tech / game-adjacent pros**, and soft intros.

**Position as:** solo / small-team product craft + intentional design — not hype trailer energy.

Good angles:

- Building a deckbuilder where **HP = your deck**
- Shipping a **web vertical slice** before Steam
- Design lesson: continuous run (explore → battle → XP → deck)
- Honest status: niche ambition, polish over feature sprawl

Bad angles:

- “Revolutionary AI game”
- Long lore dumps
- “Looking for players!!!” with no hook or clip

**Post shape:**

1. Hook line (deck is life)
2. 3–5 lines on what players do in Hollowfort
3. 15–30s clip or one strong screenshot
4. Soft CTA: “Looking for ~20 playtesters — link / comment”

English primary; Russian / Serbian as secondary posts if that matches the network.

LinkedIn seeds **credibility + playtester recruit**. Discord / itch / short video convert actual players.

### Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Positioning tone |
|---------|------|------------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Credibility, playtester recruit | Craft, hook, soft CTA |
| **Discord** | Retention / feedback | Community, patch notes, “what broke” |
| **itch / web demo page** | Conversion | Hook + short GIF + “~X min escape” |
| **Twitter/X / Bluesky** | Discovery clips | One mechanic gif + hook |
| **Reddit** (e.g. r/roguelikes, r/deckbuilding) | Honest critique | Show rules, ask specific feedback |
| **YouTube Shorts / TikTok** | Reach | Visual: cards burning as damage; map → battle hand carry |
| **Steam** | Later growth | Only when wishlistable — not required for first announce |

### Messaging by stage

| Stage | Message |
|-------|---------|
| **Pre-beta / dev diary** | “Building Hollowfort — a prison escape where your deck *is* your life.” Weekly clip + one mechanic. |
| **Closed beta** | “Need players who like Spire / Inscryption mood. Play 30–45 min, tell us where you quit.” |
| **Public beta** | “Hollowfort Prison is playable in browser. Escape once. Then pick another side.” |

Every post should answer: *What is unique? How long? How do I play? What do you want from me?*

### Don’ts

- Don’t lead with tech stack (Next.js / Nest) on player channels; LinkedIn can mention it once as craft.
- Don’t list every system (classes, XP, map, i18n…). One hook + one fantasy.
- Don’t announce “full game.” Say **vertical slice / first adventure**.
- Don’t apologize (“small indie, rough”). Be confident and specific about what is polished.

### Announce kit (prepare before posting)

1. **One sentence** — deck is life
2. **One ~20s clip** of a signature moment (damage = cards lost, or explore → battle)
3. **One still** of Hollowfort map or card hand
4. **Play link** + **feedback link**
5. **Expected session length** (e.g. “~40 min to first escape”)

### LinkedIn opener (template)

> Most deckbuilders separate “your life” from “your cards.”  
> I’m building one where **your deck is your HP**.  
>  
> Hollowfort Prison (browser beta): explore the fortress, fight with the same hand you traveled with, earn class XP, and choose a faction to escape.  
>  
> Looking for playtesters who enjoy dark fantasy deckbuilders — DM or use the link. One honest note on where you got stuck beats ten likes.
